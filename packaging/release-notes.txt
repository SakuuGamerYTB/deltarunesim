Download the archive for your computer: macos-arm64 for Apple Silicon, macos-x64 for Intel Macs, or windows-x64 for Windows 64-bit. Extract the entire folder, then launch Launch.command on macOS or DeltaruneSim.exe on Windows. Keep the launcher terminal open while playing.

Python and the game files are bundled. No additional installation or connection to the original site is required. The launchers are not publisher-signed or notarized; your operating system may display a warning or block them.

Each archive is built and smoke-tested on its target platform. Mac Chrome checks cover startup, a battle, music and rewind; not every fight or Windows graphical gameplay has been tested. External beatmap downloads require Internet, and public statistics are a frozen snapshot.

Documentation and credits are in README.md. SHA-256 checksum files accompany the archives.
